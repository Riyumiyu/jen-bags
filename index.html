        How to use this page

        When a bag sells

        1. Find the bag. Scroll, or type its number or name in the search box.
        2. If the buyer bargained, type the new price in the ₱ box first.
        3. Tick the box on the right.
        4. Pick how they paid: Cash, GCash, Other (type it, like Maya), or N/A if you don't know.
        5. Add a note if you want, like the buyer's name or GCash reference number. Tap Mark sold.

        Selling with the QR sticker

        Tap Scan QR to sell and point the camera at each bag the buyer picked. They add up in a list with the total. Change a price if the buyer bargained, or tap × to take a bag off. Pick the payment once and tap Mark sold. Then tap Next buyer. Scanning a sticker with the phone camera also works.

        Changing prices of a group

        Open Change prices of a group under the search box. Type the current price and the new one, for example ₱50 to ₱40, and tap the button. Only unsold bags change. Tap Undo to change them back.

        Changing the helper PIN

        Use Change the PIN at the top of this page. Type the current PIN and the new one twice. Every other phone asks for the new PIN once. Use Forget the PIN on this phone on a phone that shouldn't make changes anymore.

        The first time on a phone

        The page asks for the helper PIN once. Type it and tap Save PIN. Without the PIN, changes stay on that phone only and don't save.

        Fixing mistakes

        • Undo: after any change, tap Undo in the bar at the bottom, or ↶ Undo last change under the totals.
        • Wrong payment: tap Change payment under a sold bag.
        • Not really sold: untick the box, then tap Yes, unmark.

        Taking a bag out of the sale

        Tap Remove from sale, add a reason if you want, and tap Remove bag. It gets crossed out and left out of the totals. Leave "Renumber" off if the price tags are printed. Tap Put back to return it.

        Adding a new bag

        Scroll to Add a bag. Type a description, a bag number, and the price. Add a photo if you want, then tap Add bag.

        Finding bags

        • Use the buttons: All, Not sold, Sold, Removed.
        • Hide bags sold before today keeps the list short. Totals still count every sale.

        Invoice

        In Sales log, pick which sales to include and the invoice date, then tap Export invoice PDF. If it doesn't download, tap the link that appears to open it. Works best in Chrome or Safari, not inside Messenger.

        Marking an invoice paid

        Once the money for an invoice has been paid, go to Sales log, pick the same sales in Invoice covers, set the date it was paid in Invoice date, and tap Mark invoice as paid. Those sales get a Paid tag. Pick Unpaid sales to make an invoice of only what is still owed. Mark as unpaid and Undo fix a mistake. Paid marks are saved on this phone only for now.

        Statistics by date

        On Statistics, pick From and Up to dates and choose All sales, Unpaid only or Paid only. Bags sold, money collected, the 30% cut and the charts all follow your choice. Paid and unpaid amounts are shown side by side.

        Totals at the top

        • Collected: money from bags sold.
        • Unsold value: what the bags left would bring in.
        • You keep (70%): your share of the money collected. 30% cut taken: the part that goes to the other side.

        Everything saves to the Google Sheet, so every phone sees the same list within a few seconds.
